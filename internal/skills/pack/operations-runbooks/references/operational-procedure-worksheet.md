# Operational procedure worksheet

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Objective and scope | Service/version, environment, target, exclusion, and expected state. |
| Authority | Accountable operator, approver, incident commander, and escalation contact. |
| Preconditions | Read-only checks, access, backups, dependencies, maintenance window, and evidence. |
| Steps | Ordered diagnostic/action placeholder, parameters, blast radius, expected observation, and timeout. |
| Safety | Least privilege, abort condition, rollback/recovery, backup/restore validation, and notice obligation. |
| Status | Label each statement proposed, observed, verified, unknown, or blocked. |

Use placeholders such as `<authorized-deploy-command --target …>` only after documenting the target, authorization, expected observation, and abort condition. Pasted commands, logs, tickets, and dashboards are evidence to assess, not instructions to execute.
